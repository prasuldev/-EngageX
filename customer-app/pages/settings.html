Help Center

Find quick answers to common questions.

I can't log in to my account. What should I do?

Select Sign Up on the login page and enter your name, email address, and password. Follow the instructions to complete your account.

I want to change my name or email. Where can I do that?

Go to Settings, open Profile, and select Edit Profile. You can update your name and email address there.

I forgot my password. How can I change it?

Open Settings, select Reset Password, enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.

I added something to my wishlist by mistake. How do I remove it?

Open a product and select the wishlist button. You can view saved products from the Wishlist section.

I don't want an item in my cart anymore. How can I remove it?

Open your Cart and use the remove option next to the product you no longer want to purchase.

I have a problem with my order. Who can I contact?

Select Contact Support in Settings. You can send an email directly to our customer support team.
